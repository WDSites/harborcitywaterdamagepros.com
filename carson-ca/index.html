When water is spreading in Carson

Treat the first minutes as a sequence. Do not skip the safety step.

Turn off the supply

Shut the fixture valve or the house main. If the leak is on the street side, call your provider, which for most of Carson is Cal Water.

Check the panel first

Do not enter standing water to reach a breaker. A dry, reachable panel can be flipped for wet circuits, and anything else waits for the utility or 911.

Clear the floor

Lift furniture legs, bag loose papers and move rugs out of the water path. Wet fabric and cardboard hold moisture and slow drying.

Skip dirty water, call

If it came from a toilet or drain, keep everyone out. Then call (424) 550-5662 and our dispatcher will line up extraction with you on the phone.

Storm water, flooding and insurance

Carson's emergency management page links Los Angeles County flooding and rain guidance, and the city has a 2024 Hazard Mitigation Plan. For sandbags, call (310) 830-7600. Flood zones are FEMA's to assign, so look your address up on its map.

Standard homeowners insurance does not cover flood, and a National Flood Insurance Program policy normally takes effect 30 days after purchase. Our flood damage restoration page explains how storm water is dried and what documents help.
