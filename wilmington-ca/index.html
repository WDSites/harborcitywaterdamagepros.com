When water is spreading in Wilmington

Older plumbing can fail in more than one place at once. Work fast, and keep notes as you go.

Close the closest valve

Turn off the fixture stop, then the main for the unit or house. In a multi-unit building, tell the manager so the riser can be shut for everyone below.

Power only if safe

Flip breakers for wet rooms only when the panel is dry and easy to reach. If any water touches outlets or the panel, stay away and phone the utility or 911.

Lift and photograph

Take pictures of the water line, then raise books, shoes and electronics. Photos taken before you move anything help your insurer see what happened.

Stay out, then ring us

Keep children and pets away from drain or toilet water. Then call (424) 550-5662, the 24/7 line, so we can plan extraction while the building is still wet.

Renters, landlords and shared walls

With 59.3 percent of homes rented in this combined area, many Wilmington water losses start in a unit where the person on the phone does not own the building. Tell your landlord in writing, keep the message, and ask who will authorize the work.

If water came from upstairs, the neighbor's own plumbing is usually the source, and the ceiling below may need to be opened to dry the cavity. That work falls under water mitigation, and rebuilding the ceiling follows later under water damage repair.

Water, sewer and city offices

We have not verified which agency supplies water or sewer service to every Wilmington address, so ask your water utility. LADWP lists a water main break line at 1-800-DIAL-DWP for the City of Los Angeles, and LA Sanitation and Environment lists 1-800-773-CITY and 311 as contact lines.

Wilmington is in Council District 15 and has a Wilmington Neighborhood Council. For permits, the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety lists an office it calls San Pedro, at 638 S. Beacon St, which serves the Harbor area. Permit questions go to LADBS.

Wet electrical or sewage

If water reaches a panel, outlet or gas appliance, or if sewage is involved, stay out. Any electrical or gas concern gets reported to the utility or 911 before anything else.
