Water Damage and Mold Guides for Harbor City, CA

Fifteen guides written for people standing in a wet room with questions. Each one explains what to do, what to ask and where a local fact changes the answer.

How to use these guides

The guides are grouped by what is wrong: floods and burst pipes, ceilings and floors, mold and insurance. Read the one that matches your situation, then call the 24/7 line if the water is still spreading or the damage is bigger than a small spill.

Every guide includes a section on Harbor City details that comes from public sources such as LADWP, LA Sanitation, the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety and the California Department of Insurance. Where a local fact is not available we say to ask your utility or building department instead of guessing.

None of these guides replaces an on-site assessment. Moisture readings, thermal imaging and a look behind the wall change the answer.
