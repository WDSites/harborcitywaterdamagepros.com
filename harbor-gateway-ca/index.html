Water Damage Cleanup in Harbor Gateway, CA

Burst line, backed-up drain or storm water in Harbor Gateway? This page explains the first steps and which City of Los Angeles offices apply.

☎ Call (424) 550-5662

Water damage cleanup in Harbor Gateway means removing standing water from a home or building, drying the structure behind the finishes, and taking out materials that cannot be dried. Harbor Gateway runs along the north side of Harbor City, where our address is.

As a City of Los Angeles neighborhood, it uses the city's water, sanitation and building offices, which are listed below along with a few tips for describing your location.

Where Harbor Gateway is and how to describe your address

Los Angeles City Planning describes Harbor Gateway as lying south of 120th Street and north of Sepulveda Boulevard, covering about 3,229 acres. It calls the area a narrow corridor linking the harbor area to the main body of the city, bordering Southeast Los Angeles, Wilmington and Harbor City, and the cities of Gardena, Torrance and Carson.

A long, thin neighborhood is hard to place from a name alone. When you call, give the nearest cross streets, and say whether the water is in a house, a duplex, an apartment building or a business, so the dispatcher can plan correctly.

The neighborhood council for the northern part, Harbor Gateway North, describes its own boundary as running down to Artesia Boulevard on the south side. If you are unsure which part of the neighborhood you live in, your cross streets are a better guide than a council name.

Housing mix and who handles the repair

City Planning says the updated Harbor LA community plans aim to keep stable single-family and multi-family residential neighborhoods. That matches what the name suggests: houses, duplexes and apartment buildings on the same corridor, often a few doors apart.

The practical effect is on responsibility. A homeowner closes the shutoff, calls the insurer and approves the repair. A tenant should photograph the damage and tell the landlord or property manager at once, because the building owner usually controls the plumbing and the repair schedule.

We do not claim a type of damage is more common here than elsewhere, because no local source we reviewed measures that. What we can say is that water travels through shared walls and floors in multi-unit buildings, so a leak that looks small in one unit can reach another.

Safety in the first hour in Harbor Gateway

Close the water supply

Shut the fixture valve or the main valve at the house if you can reach it safely. A main break in the street is a utility problem, and LADWP lists 1-800-DIAL-DWP (1-800-342-5397) for it.

Keep clear of electrical hazards

Water around outlets, a panel or appliances means leaving the area and calling 911 or the electric utility. Touch a breaker only from dry footing.

Avoid sewage and street water

If the water came up through a drain or toilet, stay out and keep pets away. LA Sanitation and Environment lists 1-800-773-2489 and the 311 line.

Document, then call us

Once the room is safe, photograph what you see, raise valuables off the floor and call (424) 550-5662 for the next step.

Storms, street drains and what the record shows

Regional storm reports are not neighborhood damage reports. KABC and City News Service reported that, as of the evening of February 20, 2024, the City of Los Angeles had received nearly 100 major flooding reports tied to blocked storm basins, with a little more than half cleared. The report did not name Harbor Gateway, so we do not claim the neighborhood was affected.

The useful lesson is practical: leaves and debris at a street drain can send water toward a doorway during heavy rain. Clear what you safely can in dry weather, and report a blocked basin to the city. Water that has already entered the home is the part we handle.

How the cleanup and drying work

The work begins with moisture meter readings and a thermal imaging camera pass, followed by extraction of standing water. Drying equipment such as air movers and LGR dehumidifiers then runs for several days, while soaked carpet pad, insulation and softened drywall generally come out.

Expect an explanation of what comes out, and why, before work starts. For repair permits, ask LADBS directly, since its plumbing permit page covers drainage, waste and vent, gas and potable water piping.

Insurance and records

Whether insurance helps depends on the cause and the policy, so read your policy and ask your insurer. The state insurance department's flood guide says standard homeowners policies leave flood out, and insurers tend to judge a sudden pipe failure differently from a slow leak.

Dated photos, readings and a written scope make a record you can hand over, although a record alone does not decide whether a claim is paid. Keep receipts and any failed parts.

Services for a Harbor Gateway loss

Harbor Gateway details worth knowing

The City Council adopted the updated Harbor LA community plans on September 9, 2025. The Harbor Gateway plan had last been updated in 1995, so planning documents for the area are recent, but they describe land use and not water damage history.

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status was not verified for Harbor Gateway. Search your own address on FEMA's flood map before relying on any zone label.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water damage in Harbor Gateway?

Yes, call (424) 550-5662, name the nearest cross streets and say what happened. Availability for your address is confirmed on that call, and no arrival time is promised in advance.

Is Harbor Gateway the same as Harbor City?

No. Both are City of Los Angeles neighborhoods, but City Planning places Harbor Gateway north of Sepulveda Boulevard and Harbor City to its south.

Who do I call if the water is a main break in the street?

LADWP's news site lists 1-800-DIAL-DWP (1-800-342-5397) for main breaks. The utility fixes the main, and water that has come into your building is the cleanup we handle.

My apartment has water coming through the ceiling. What now?

Tell the landlord or property manager right away, photograph the stain and move belongings away from it. If the ceiling sags or drips near light fixtures, keep clear and call us for a moisture check.

Does a bigger loss always mean replacing everything?

No. Clean water caught early often lets floors and framing be dried in place. Sewage, long-standing water and soaked insulation are more likely to be removed.
