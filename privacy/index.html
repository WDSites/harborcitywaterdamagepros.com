Privacy Policy

Who we are

Harbor City Water Damage Pros, 1403 W Lomita Blvd, Harbor City, CA 90710, operates this website. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

How you contact us

This website has no forms. You reach us by phone at (424) 550-5662 or by email at contact@harborcitywaterdamagepros.com. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you call or email, we use the details you give us, such as your name, address and a description of the damage, to respond to your request and to plan the work.

Website data

Like most sites, the hosting service may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the site running and secure.

This site does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to do the work you request, for example with crews and with your insurer when you ask us to provide documentation. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by calling or emailing us. California residents may have additional rights under state law.

We may update this policy, and the current version on this page always applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@harborcitywaterdamagepros.com or by calling (424) 550-5662. You can also read our terms of use.
