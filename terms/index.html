General information only

The content on this website is general information about water damage, flooding and mold. It is not legal, insurance, engineering or medical advice. Conditions in your building differ, so an on-site assessment is needed before any scope or price is set.

Services and pricing

Services are provided under a written scope of work that we explain before work starts. Prices depend on the factors in that scope, such as the water category, area affected, materials, drying time and rebuild work. Descriptions on this site are not an offer or a quote.

Insurance

We do not approve insurance claims or promise what a policy will pay. Coverage is decided by your insurer. We can provide photos, moisture readings and a written scope for you to share with your insurer.

Response times

Our emergency line is answered 24 hours a day. Same-day response is offered when available, and no arrival time is guaranteed.

Calls

Changes
