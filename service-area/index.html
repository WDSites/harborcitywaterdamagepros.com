Where the crews work

Our address is 1403 W Lomita Blvd, Harbor City, CA 90710, and the dispatch line covers the surrounding South Bay and Harbor area around the clock. Each town below has different water utilities, different permit offices and different housing, so each gets its own page instead of a shared template.

If your town is not listed, call anyway. Same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher will confirm whether a crew can reach you.

How dispatch works across the area

A dispatcher takes your address, the source of the water and the rooms affected. Crew availability depends on where the nearest crew is and how many emergencies are in progress, so we do not promise an arrival time. We do offer same-day response when available and we tell you plainly what to expect.

While you wait, the best thing you can do is stop the source. Close the fixture valve or the main water valve, keep people and pets out of any water that may be contaminated and photograph the damage. Our emergency water removal page lists the full first-hour checklist.

After the emergency stage, the same crew can continue into water mitigation and drying, then cleanup and repair. Each town page lists local contacts such as the building department so you know who to ask about permits.

Each town page lists its own ZIP codes, the local building department or utility contacts we could verify, and the practical details that change a water job there, such as older housing, shared walls or storm drain routes. Read the page for your town before you call so you can describe your property accurately.

When you phone, have three things ready: where the water came from, whether it has stopped, and which rooms are wet. The dispatcher uses those answers to decide what to send, and the written scope and price are explained before any work begins. Start with our services overview if you are unsure which kind of help fits, or go straight to emergency water removal when water is still spreading.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve my town if it is not listed?

Call and ask. Dispatch decides by where a crew can reach you the same day, and the line is answered 24/7.

Why do some towns list more than one ZIP code?

Larger places such as Torrance and Carson have several street-delivery ZIP codes. Each town page names every ZIP we serve there.

Is Harbor City its own city?

No. Harbor City is a neighborhood of the City of Los Angeles in Los Angeles County, with its own ZIP code, 90710.

Water is spreading right now?

Call the 24/7 dispatch line. Describe where the water is coming from and which rooms are wet, and the dispatcher will tell you what to do while a crew is arranged.
