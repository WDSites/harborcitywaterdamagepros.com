Emergency response

Emergency water damage in Harbor City: what to do before the crew arrives

A 24/7 emergency line matters because water does not wait for business hours. Every hour that drywall, insulation and carpet pad stay wet adds to the repair, and the first 24 to 48 hours is also the window before mold has much to work with.

The four steps below can be done while you wait for a crew, as long as it is safe to reach them.

Stop the source

Close the shut-off valve at the fixture or the main water valve for the house. If the break is on the street side of the meter, LADWP lists 1-800-DIAL-DWP for water main breaks.

Power comes first

If water has reached outlets, appliances or the panel, stay out of the room. Cut power only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground, and call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

Move what you can

Lift small furniture onto blocks, pull rugs and electronics out of the wet area and photograph everything first. Leave heavy pieces where they are.

Keep people and pets out

Water from a toilet, a sewer line or outside flooding can carry bacteria. Keep children and pets away, and do not run a household vacuum on standing water.

Call the 24/7 emergency line

Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from, which rooms are wet and whether power is safe. The crew plan starts from those three answers.

Know before you call

What affects the cost of water damage restoration

Cost factorHow it changes the scope
Water categoryClean water from a supply line is the simplest. Gray water and sewage or floodwater add removal of porous materials and antimicrobial treatment.
Affected areaMore square footage and more rooms mean more extraction, more equipment and more monitoring visits.
Materials involvedCarpet pad, drywall and insulation hold water and are often removed. Hardwood, tile and concrete are more likely to be dried in place.
Drying daysEquipment runs until moisture readings reach the dry standard for each material, which commonly takes several days.
Rebuild scopeDrywall, paint, flooring and cabinets are repair work that is scoped separately once the structure is dry.

Insurance is a separate conversation. Sudden and accidental water damage, such as a burst pipe, is often covered by a homeowners policy, while a slow leak or neglected maintenance often is not. Flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage, so check your policy or ask your insurer.
